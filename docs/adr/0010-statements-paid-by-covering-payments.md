# Statements are paid by the payments that cover them

A Statement can have many payments, since people often pay a card in parts, and it is paid only once the payments linked to it add up to its Statement Amount. Linking any payment no longer counts as paying it, because a part payment would then hide what is still owed. Each payment pays at most one Statement, so splitting one payment across Statements is not supported. A Statement keeps a single payment Draft: while unpaid, the waiting Draft proposes what is still owed, but once that Draft is posted no second one is proposed, and anything still owed shows only as the Statement being unpaid.

Every payment into the card dated after a Statement's close links itself to the oldest unpaid Statement, whatever its amount or source Account; what goes beyond that Statement's remaining amount becomes Carried Credit. Velora matched only one payment within ±10% of what was owed, so part payments and payments from another bank needed linking by hand, and that is where most of its card bugs came from. The user can still Link or Unlink a payment by hand to override the rule.

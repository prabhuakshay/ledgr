# Valued Accounts and Term Deposits are worth more than their Balance

A house, a car or an FD changes in worth without any money moving, so its Balance alone makes Net Worth drift from reality. A Valued Account keeps its Balance as the money that went into it and carries dated Valuations beside it; Net Worth counts its Value, the latest Valuation plus money moved since, as it counts an Investment Account at Market Value (ADR 0012). Revaluation Transactions to an Expense or Income Account were set aside: they keep Net Worth equal to the sum of Balances, but put non-cash guesses into Spent, Budgets and reports, which ledgr keeps to real money. A Term Deposit's growth is known in advance, so its Value is worked out from its rate and compounding rather than typed in as Valuations. Its interest is posted only at maturity, as a Draft from an Income Account into the deposit, so Income is recorded when the bank pays it and the Balance matches the bank's figure for Reconciliation; Pay out and Renew then follow from that Balance. PPF and EPF stay ordinary Asset Accounts, since their interest is credited yearly and the passbook is the record to match.
